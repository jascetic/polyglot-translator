How your content is handled

Polyglot is a single web page with no accounts and no database. Here is exactly what happens to what you translate.

Files open on your device

PDF, Word and image files are read and rebuilt inside your browser. The files themselves are never uploaded anywhere.

Who translates it

In Chrome on a computer, supported languages are translated on your device and the text stays there. Otherwise the text is sent to MyMemory, a free translation service, which allows about 5,000 characters a day per connection.

Nothing kept

The page remembers only your target language and today's character count, in your own browser. Your text is not saved.

Check what matters

Machine translation can make mistakes. Have legal, medical or official documents checked by a qualified translator before you rely on them.
